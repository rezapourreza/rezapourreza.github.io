Senior Staff Engineer · Qualcomm AI Research

LinkedInGoogle ScholarXGmail

I am a Senior Staff Engineer at Qualcomm AI Research. My research focuses on multimodal learning and reasoning, including streaming video-language models, real-world interactive assistants, and detecting and reducing hallucinations in large language models.

Previously, I worked on neural image and video compression, computational photography, and medical image analysis. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from The University of Texas at Dallas in 2016, and my M.Sc. and B.Sc. from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad in 2008 and 2005. Before joining Qualcomm, I worked at the National Institutes of Health and Philips Healthcare.

I am a co-inventor on 18+ granted U.S. patents.

Research interests: Multimodal AI · Visual reasoning · Reliable language models · Neural compression
